# Arithi Brand Assets

Official logos, icons and brand files for **Arithi**, India's STEM learning and competitions platform.

You'll find the same assets, with previews, at **[arithi.in/brand-assets](https://arithi.in/brand-assets)**.

![Arithi logo](logos/primary-plum-sage-on-cream/png/arithi-lockup-on-bg-1200w.png)

**[Download everything (.zip)](https://github.com/Arithi-Official/arithi-brand/archive/refs/heads/main.zip)**

## The logo

- **Mark:** the "orbit crossbar" A, a letter A with an orbit ring around it.
- **Wordmark:** ARiTHi, with rounded letters.
- **Lockup:** the mark and the wordmark together. Use the lockup by default.
- **Mark or app icon:** use these on their own where space is tight (avatars, favicons, app icons).

| Name  | Hex       | Use |

| Sage  | `#CDECD3` | Orbit ring, accents |

## Approved colourways

| Folder | Colours | When to use |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| `logos/primary-plum-sage-on-cream` | Plum and sage on cream | **The default. Use it everywhere.** |
| `logos/plum-cream-on-sage` | Plum and cream on sage | On sage backgrounds |
| `logos/plum-sage-on-blush` | Plum and sage on blush | On blush backgrounds |
| `logos/plum-cream-on-peach` | Plum and cream on peach | On peach backgrounds |
| `logos/single-colour-plum` | Plum only | Print, stamps, merch, embroidery, and any other background |

Each colourway folder has:

- **`svg/`:**
  - `arithi-lockup.svg`
  - `arithi-lockup-on-bg.svg`
  - `arithi-mark.svg`
  - `arithi-icon.svg`
- **`png/`:**
  - The lockup at 600, 1200 and 2400px wide, transparent or on its background.
  - The mark at 1024px.
  - App icons at 32, 180, 512 and 1024px.

## Other folders

- **`website/`:**
  - Favicons (`.ico`, `.svg`, 16, 32 and 48px).
  - The Apple touch icon.
  - Android, PWA and maskable icons, plus `site.webmanifest`.
  - The Open Graph image.
  - Header and footer logos, plus `head-snippet.html`.
- **`social/`:** profile pictures (circle-safe), plus headers and posts for LinkedIn, X, YouTube, Facebook and Instagram.
- **`print-and-office/`:** email signature, letterhead logo, single-colour print files, stamp and merch mark, and a presentation title slide.

## Usage rules

- **Use SVG where you can.** Use PNG only where SVG isn't accepted.
- **Match the colourway to its background.** The right leg of the A is filled with the background colour, so each colourway only works on its own background. On any other background, use **single-colour plum**.
- **Clear space:** leave at least the height of the A's dot free on every side.
- **Minimum size:** 120px wide for the lockup, 16px for the icon.
- **Don't change the logo.** Don't recolour, stretch, rotate, or crop it. Don't add shadows, outlines, gradients or effects. Don't retype the wordmark in a font.
- **Don't imply a relationship that doesn't exist.** Don't use the logo in a way that suggests Arithi sponsors, endorses or partners with you, unless it does.
